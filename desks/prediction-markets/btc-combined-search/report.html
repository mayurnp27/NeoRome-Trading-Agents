What was combined

Stochastic parameters were frozen to the preceding validation selections: 5m (48,5,5) K/D crossover; 15m (5,1,1) K turning up from ≤20 or down from ≥80; 30m (8,3,1) with the same turn rule. SMA lengths were 2/5 and 5/10 candles, each on 5m, 15m and 30m charts.

All 18 stochastic-chart × SMA-chart × SMA-pair assignments were tested for each of three two-indicator recipes, plus 36 stochastic-event confirmations using SMA states on two different charts. All inputs must agree. A direction state is not a new crossover: stochastic state means K-D sign for the 5m family and K-slope sign for the turn families; SMA state means fast above/below slow. These controls are explicitly distinct from simultaneous events.

Exploratory high score—not the selected winner

After looking across the final-period results, the highest accuracy among combinations with at least 100 signals was 56.63% across 249 signals: stoch:15m:event, sma:5m:2/5:state, sma:30m:5/10:state. This was found by inspecting the evaluation results and is not independent evidence of an edge. It was not the validation-selected winner and should not be substituted for it. It needs a new prospective test.

Evaluation boundaries

Reused 17,027 official Kalshi outcomes from April 21–October 17 UTC and the same Coinbase BTC-USD candles. Final evaluation: September 13–October 17 UTC, 3,320 markets. This period was inspected in earlier studies; results are exploratory, not fresh holdout evidence. The models were locked before this run's final metrics, but their component choices inherit earlier research exposure.

Signals use only completed bars at quarter-hour market starts. Events between starts are not carried forward; chart states may be. A 30m event only triggers at alternate starts. Missing windows are excluded. Entry-time availability, asks, spreads, fees, depth and fills are unmodeled; there is no net-profit claim.

Selection required at least 200 training and 100 validation signals and ranked validation correlation. Final intervals use 2,000 daily-block resamples and are not multiple-comparison-adjusted. Signals below 100 final occurrences are sparse. Agreement filters retain the same directional predictions as their components on overlapping trades; their effect is selection of opportunities, not a new independent prediction on those trades. The paired comparisons use the same valid market universe, while the traded subsets differ.

Verification: five focused tests, independent scalar agreement/count/accuracy/correlation checks for all 90 combinations, all 15 prior event/SMA-state baseline matches, and five validation-selection checks. No trading services, accounts or rules changed.
