Predict the hourly market result, enter at least 30 minutes before close

The target is the contract’s displayed price-to-beat or strike. The BTC price at our entry is not the settlement benchmark, and the contract’s purchase price in cents is a separate cost.

Decisions at minutes 0, 15 and 29. Modeled execution exactly one minute later, at minutes 1, 16 and 30: respectively 59, 44 and 30 minutes remain. Late replacement fills are excluded. Only completed chart candles available at decision time are used.

The benchmark used

MarketSettlement benchmarkResult being predicted
Kalshi KXBTCD Above/BelowListed dollar strike of the selected contractYES if final 60-second BRTI average is strictly above the strike; NO otherwise.
Polymarket hourly Up/DownOpening trade of that hour’s Binance BTC/USDT candleUP if hourly close is greater than or equal to hourly open; DOWN otherwise.

Example: a $100,000 benchmark and a $100,100 settlement is an Above/Up outcome even if BTC was $100,400 when the contract was purchased. That intervening fall does not change the benchmark.

What the deadline changes

A signal based on the candle finishing at minute 30 cannot be assumed available for a delayed fill by minute 30. The latest decision here is minute 29. It therefore cannot use the new 30-minute candle, or the 15-minute candle closing at minute 30. With completed candles only, its indicator states equal those available at minute 15; fresh crossover/zone events do not fire at minute 29. Any accuracy increase from using an unfinished candle must be tested separately with point-in-time partial-bar construction.

Method and limits

Same 180-day data and original official outcome labels. Training April 21–August 6; August 7 embargo; validation August 8–September 11; September 12 embargo; evaluation September 13–October 17. Training/validation labels must settle before their cutoffs. This evaluation period was already inspected repeatedly: exploratory, not an untouched confirmation.

Stochastic lookback 5–100, K/D smoothing 1–5, eight rules and both orientations. SMA every pair 2≤fast<slow≤200, state and fresh cross, both orientations. Four chart timeframes and three decisions: 1,406,448 single-chart variants per target, 2,812,896 total. This includes repeated minute-0/15 variants from the original study. Training-only top ten distinct sequences per family/chart/time; top-five pair combinations and top-one three/four-chart agreements. Minimum 200 training and 50 validation signals. Validation signed correlation ranks candidates and entry times. Combinations are exhaustive only within these declared shortlists.

Combination counts: Kalshi: 4,344, Polymarket: 4,344. Confidence intervals use 2,000 whole-day resamples over only 35 evaluation days and are unadjusted for the large search. Original final metadata can contain historical revisions not reconstructed. Coinbase is a proxy for Kalshi’s BRTI settlement source; Binance matches the Polymarket rule.

Verification and reproducibility

Nine focused tests passed. Independent calculations reconstructed 765,180 selected signal cells and checked source precision, arithmetic bounds, chronological masks, price baselines and declared trial counts. Candidate scores for every trial were not independently recomputed. Exact-rational audit checked 1,796 trades and 3,592 cash events. All saved account decisions and trades meet the at-least-30-minute deadline in modeled timestamps.
