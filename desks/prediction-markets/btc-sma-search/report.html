Exact rule

Fast SMA crossing from at/below to above the slow SMA predicts UP; crossing from at/above to below predicts DOWN. Otherwise abstain. Use fully closed candles at the 15-minute market opening. Five-minute crossovers between market openings are not carried into later contracts; 30-minute crossover events occur only at alternate openings. No intramarket entry or stale event reuse.

For the 15-minute 5/10 candidate: UP calls won 107/198 (54.04%); DOWN calls won 100/196 (51.02%). Always-UP on the same 394 selected markets would have achieved 51.52%, versus 52.54% for the signal. Correlation is 0.0175, with 95% daily-block interval −0.0195 to 0.0537.

Controls and limitations

The separate above/below state controls scored 48.49–50.33% in the final period; merely staying above a moving average is not a crossover. Normal bullish/bearish interpretation was fixed in advance; no inversion or parameter optimization. An alternate literal-days diagnostic was computed while units were being clarified and is retained only in the machine-readable record, excluded from this requested six-case comparison.

One-day chronological split embargoes, missing-window exclusion, closed-bar alignment, 2,000 daily-block bootstrap samples, five focused tests, and independent scalar reconstruction of all six cases. Initial test fixture assertions were corrected to the hand-calculated crossing bars; the vectorized indicator matched the scalar formula. Intervals are descriptive and not multiplicity-adjusted. Coinbase chart history differs from the CF Benchmarks settlement feed and may not match TradingView exactly. Boundary data latency, asks, fees, depth and fills are not modeled. No economic profitability established and no trading settings changed.
