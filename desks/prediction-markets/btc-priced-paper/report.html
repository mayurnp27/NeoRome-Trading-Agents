Why 93% accuracy still lost money

Strategy C predicted 1,495 of 1,605 eligible outcomes correctly (93.15%). Buying one contract on every signal would have cost an average 93.632¢ plus 0.370¢ in fees: approximately a 94.00% break-even win rate. The realized average was a loss of 0.855¢ per contract. The loss remained negative before fees. A high probability of winning was already reflected in the purchase price.

Execution rules and evidence

A follows the side of the original settlement strike at minute 5. B follows it at minute 10. C uses minute 10 only when absolute normalized lead is at least 1: log(current Coinbase price / strike) divided by the standard deviation of the last twelve completed five-minute log returns, scaled by the square root of remaining five-minute units. These rules and the threshold were fixed before inspecting the quote-priced returns.

The main replay buys at the close of the Kalshi minute candle ending 60 seconds after the signal. YES uses the ask; NO uses one minus the YES bid. It requires an exact quote timestamp, valid noncrossed prices, and an entry before market close. Whole contracts are sized to the largest quantity whose premium plus fees fits 25% of settled cash. Only one position may be open per account. Official settlement_ts controls when a payout becomes spendable.

The modeled taker fee is 0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price), rounded upward to six decimals, with the total buyer debit aligned upward to $0.0001 for a direct member. Cent balance precision is separately tested. There is no settlement fee. Archived series metadata specifies a quadratic fee and multiplier 1; the historical series fee-change response is empty, and none of the 19 event fee overrides returned applies to KXBTC15M.

Sources: Kalshi fee schedule, effective July 7, 2026; fee rounding; bid/ask candlesticks API; series fee history; event fee overrides.

Validation and limits

All 3,320 market quote histories were retrieved in 166 successful cached requests. Eight focused tests passed. An independent audit using exact rational arithmetic reconciled 7,334 trades and 14,668 cash events across all 15 scenarios, including quote side/time, signal, fee, maximum size, payout, and cash chronology. Independent Luna review found no accounting or timing defect and requested full input hashes, now included.

This reuses a period already inspected during earlier stochastic/SMA research. It is not fresh out-of-sample confirmation. Coinbase candles are not Kalshi's BRTI settlement feed; original strike publication times are not archived. The one-minute delay does not reconstruct tick-level latency. Candle quotes do not prove the quoted size was fillable. The data are the prior eligible market subset, not every possible market. The conclusion is limited to these rules, this period, and these fill assumptions.

Research decision: none of the three rules qualifies as a profitable strategy on this evidence. The next useful experiment would forecast settlement probability, compare it with executable cost after fees, and permit a trade only for a calibrated positive price edge. It needs fresh prospective quote/depth observations and a separate sizing decision; it should not tune against these already inspected returns.
